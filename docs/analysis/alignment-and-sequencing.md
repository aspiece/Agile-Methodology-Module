# Agile Methodology: Alignment and Sequencing Findings

## Source boundary

- Preserve the approved ten AG standards, the Student Hub continuous scenario, accurate Agile/Scrum distinctions, short scenario-based application, and mostly auto-graded assessment approach.
- Reorganize prior concepts into an eight-lesson progression from Agile foundations through integrated practice.
- Rewrite explanations and questions for grades 7–8 readability while preserving grades 11–12 CTE decisions and technical accuracy.
- Treat the Agile Manifesto and 2020 Scrum Guide as proposed authoritative additions. They require blueprint approval before learner-facing use.
- Treat acceptance-criteria instruction, the workflow visual, and the reusable Agile Project Toolkit as proposed additions needed to satisfy the standards and stated deliverables.

## Priority findings

1. The source sequence is coherent when user needs and acceptance criteria precede backlog ordering and Sprint Planning.
2. Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective need separate instruction because one inspects the product/outcome and the other inspects the team's process.
3. User stories are taught as a common Agile practice, not a required Scrum artifact.
4. The Scrum Master video has conflicting availability results and should remain supplemental until resolved.
5. Required videos need a final caption-accuracy check or equivalent transcript before release.
6. Program coverage is scoped to the enrolled Computer Science pathway. Broad cross-program variants would weaken the continuous software-development case unless this module will be shared across programs.

## Assessment plan

- Each lesson ends with a portable assessment aligned to its listed objectives. Its number follows the application with no skipped steps.
- Most items use multiple choice, multi-select, ordering, or categorization with plausible distractors.
- Authentic short responses are limited to a user story with acceptance criteria, a Sprint Goal, a prioritization rationale, and a retrospective improvement.
- The final assessment emphasizes decisions across the full workflow rather than isolated vocabulary.
- Template defaults remain 80% mastery and three attempts; no live form or LMS object will be created without separate authorization.

## Pacing

Each blueprint row totals approximately 50 minutes. Time includes required `.2` instruction, optional later instruction only where concept load requires it, a required application, and a required assessment. Steps are numbered sequentially with no gaps.
