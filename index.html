Eight-week learning module

Agile Methodology

Use Agile and Scrum practices to guide a software team as it builds Student Hub, a student facing app for assignments, deadlines, and course events. Across the module, you will turn user needs into backlog work, plan sprints, inspect feedback, improve the team process, and deliver usable product increments.

Module project

Student Hub

Student Hub is the practice product for this module. It is a simple app students could use to track assignments, deadlines, and course events. Each lesson adds one Agile decision the team needs to make as the product changes.
